Host cheat sheet

Before the night

  • Open the show on the laptop you'll use, then press Ctrl+Shift+R once for the latest version.
  • Set up Teams: names, colours, captains.
  • Pick the games: how many rounds, which game each round, how many questions.
  • Press H for the host controls → Get clips ready. Wait for “10 of 10 saved for offline”.
  • Check the questions: Settings → Edit questions and packs.
  • Memory Tray: 12 household items on a tray, plus a cloth to cover them.
  • Paper and pens for every team.
  • Optional: switch on the phone remote in the host controls and scan the QR code.

Setting up the TV

  1. Connect the laptop to the TV and choose Extend, not mirror (Windows: Win+P; Mac: Displays → untick Mirror).
  2. Drag the show window onto the TV and press F for full screen.
  3. Click the TV window once so it's allowed to make sound (it shows “Click to turn on sound” until you do).
  4. Press H: the host controls open on the laptop. They show the answers; the TV doesn't.

The one key: Space

Space (or the big button in the host controls) always does the next thing: intro → question → start timer → time's up → reveal → next question → standings → next round → winner. The host controls say what it will do.

Round by round

1 · MusicSpace plays the clip on the TV; the timer starts when it ends. Space during a clip cuts it short. A replays. Wrong version? Change it in the host controls.

2 · NauseousSpace starts the timer. X shuffles the letters if teams are stuck. The reveal spells the word.

3 · Brain GamesSpace starts the timer, then reveals. Score with 1–4.

4 · Mini GamesTurn-by-turn races: Space starts a team's turn, Space again when they finish. Last one standing: number key when a team is out. Memory Tray: Space to look, Space to write. Charades prompts are only in the host controls. Pick the winner on the result screen.

5 · Category BankSpace starts. Space = valid answer (points, next team). X = repeat or wrong (out). Out of time = out. Last team standing gets the bonus.

Tie-breakerOnly if the top two are level. Type each guess in the host controls; the reveal ranks them; give the tie-break to the closest.

Finale“And the winner is…”, confetti and final scores. Play again, new teams, or replay the reveal.

If something goes wrong

No soundClick the TV window once, and check M (mute) and the laptop's volume.
Clip won't playPlay the song from your phone, then press Space for the timer.
Wrong scoreU undoes the last change; click a team's score for custom points.
Looks out of dateCtrl+Shift+R on each window. The game and scores are kept.
No internetEverything still works, except songs not saved in advance and the phone remote.
Phone droppedIt reconnects by itself; keep the TV window open.
Wrong screen← goes back a step; Esc returns to the running order.
