Technology & Innovation

Every industrial asset should be visible, measurable and improving.

Halo Energy connects the machinery that moves projects and the infrastructure that powers industries with the data that makes them work better. Halo Connect is the name we give to that connected layer.

Explore Halo Connect View the roadmap

Our position on technology

We don't claim technology as a differentiator. We show it in delivery.

Using software does not make a company different. The difference has to be visible in how services are delivered, measured and improved.

So we start practical. The first version of Halo Connect is assembled from proven telematics, maintenance, inspection and reporting tools, configured around Halo's services and integrated into one client view. As our operating data grows, we will build proprietary capability where it adds real value — and we will say clearly which is which.

  • Proven tools firstPartner and off-the-shelf platforms before proprietary software.
  • Client data stays the client'sClear data ownership, access control and export on request.
  • Honest labellingAvailable, in development and roadmap capabilities are always marked.

Halo Connected Operations

One connected view of industrial operations.

Six modules describe what Halo Connect is designed to do. Together they turn equipment, sites and suppliers into a single, measurable operation.

Product vision. Halo Connect is a proposed platform identity and capability roadmap. It is not yet a live, commercially available software product.

Phase 1–2
Asset Visibility

A live register of every asset: where it is, who supplied it, its documents and condition.

  • Digital equipment register
  • Location via partner telematics
  • Document and certificate tracking
Phase 2
Fleet Intelligence

How hard each machine is working, where idle time sits and how fleets compare.

  • Hours, utilisation, idle time
  • Availability and downtime
  • Fleet right-sizing insight
Phase 2–3
Maintenance Intelligence

Service history, upcoming work and recurring faults — moving from reactive to planned.

  • Electronic service records
  • Inspection defects to work orders
  • Predictive maintenance pilots
Phase 3
Project Intelligence

Equipment and site activity linked to progress, so performance is seen at project level.

  • Progress against plan
  • Survey and GIS data via partners
  • Resource-to-output analysis
Phase 2–3
Operational Analytics

Consistent reporting and analysis across assets, sites, suppliers and time.

  • Scheduled client reports
  • Cost and productivity views
  • Automated alerts
Phase 1
Supplier Integration

Owners, OEMs and service providers onboarded and measured to one standard.

  • Digital supplier onboarding
  • Document control
  • Supplier performance records
