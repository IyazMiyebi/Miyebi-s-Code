Who we are

An energy and industrial partner built around visibility.

Industrial projects run on machines, sites and people spread across many suppliers. Most of what happens to those assets is recorded late, on paper, or not at all.

Halo Energy brings the physical work — sourcing and deploying equipment, supporting site and infrastructure development, coordinating maintenance — together with the digital systems that make it measurable. Our aim is simple: every asset we manage should be visible, accountable and improving.

Integrated delivery

From requirement to measurable result.

Clients should not have to manage fragmented equipment suppliers, disconnected site data and multiple reporting formats on their own. Our delivery model puts it under one line of accountability.

  1. Define

    Scope equipment, site conditions, duration and reporting needs with your project team.

  2. Source

    Match requirements to vetted owners, operators and specialist suppliers.

  3. Mobilise

    Coordinate inspection, transport, documentation and safe arrival on site.

  4. Connect

    Register each asset digitally and link telematics, inspections and service records.

  5. Improve

    Report on utilisation, uptime and cost — and act on what the data shows.
