01 · Halo Equipment

Intelligent equipment solutions.

Heavy earthmoving, swamp and pipeline equipment for energy and industrial projects — sourced, mobilised and maintained through qualified partners, with every machine recorded and reported digitally.

Submit an Equipment Enquiry Browse equipment categories

The problem

Project teams spend too much time chasing equipment: comparing owners, checking machine condition, arranging transport and then discovering — weeks later — that a unit sat idle or missed its service. Equipment is often the largest variable cost on site, yet the least visible.

Our solution

Halo Equipment acts as your managed equipment partner. We source suitable machines from vetted ownership and operating partners, coordinate inspection and mobilisation, provide operators where needed, and manage maintenance throughout the hire. You deal with one partner and one standard of documentation, regardless of how many owners supply the fleet.

Technology component

Every unit is entered in a digital equipment register with its documents, inspection history and service records. Where machines carry OEM or third-party telematics, we integrate location, hours and usage data. Clients receive regular utilisation and availability reporting. Predictive maintenance is a future capability we will pilot once sufficient equipment data is available.

Delivery model

  1. Requirement

    Equipment type, duty, terrain, duration, operated or bare.

  2. Partner match

    Shortlist from vetted owners; verify condition and papers.

  3. Mobilise

    Pre-mobilisation inspection, transport and site induction.

  4. Operate & maintain

    Daily inspections, planned service and fast breakdown response.

  5. Report

    Utilisation, availability and maintenance summaries.
